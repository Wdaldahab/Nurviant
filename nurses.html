How it works

Three steps, and the hospital does the hiring.

Nurviant is a recruiter, not an employer. You join the hospital’s staff, not a contract.

  1. 01

    Tell us your specialty and where you want to work.

    Send a note to hello@nurviant.com with your specialty, your experience, and the states or cities you’d consider. That’s enough to start.

  2. 02

    We match you with hospitals that are hiring directly.

    We look for permanent openings that fit your specialty and your location, then introduce you to the hospitals hiring for them.

  3. 03

    The hospital hires you — Nurviant’s fee is paid by the hospital, never by the nurse.

    You interview with the hospital and, when it’s a fit, you’re hired onto their staff. Our fee is settled between Nurviant and the hospital.

FAQ

Does it cost me anything?

No. Hospitals pay our fee. Nurses never pay Nurviant anything — not to start, not at offer, not after you’re hired.

What roles?

Permanent, direct-hire nursing roles with U.S. hospitals. We don’t work on travel contracts or per-diem shifts.

How do I start?

Send an inquiry to hello@nurviant.com. Tell us your specialty and where you’d like to work, and we’ll take it from there.
